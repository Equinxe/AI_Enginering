### Changed

- Every agent `generate-agents.sh` writes states the one-suite rule, in a `Test Runs` section, in the words of `Templates/EMPLOYEE_BRIEF.md`: at most one full test suite at a time, never a second one beside it in the background or fanned out. The generator reads the line from the template at each generation, so the two cannot diverge, and an Employee dispatched without a brief built from the template reads the rule too. `--check`, and the agent family of `check-framework-drift.sh`, report every agent generated before this change as stale until it is regenerated. A template that is missing, or that holds the rule's line zero times or twice, stops a generation or a full check with exit 2 and writes no agent; the quick check of session start does not read it (#373, WU-IAENG-453)
